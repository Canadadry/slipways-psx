The attract mode's demo: from a record of year 3412, a fold reveals
three planets, an iceball is colonised, a slipway links it to a
colony, the year ends, "xenofoods" is invented; then the closing
card. Seven captions, one per part, tile the whole demo. 2736 frames
(45.6 s), from the resume of the record, on the default bindings, no
cheat.

The record is cut, never typed, from worst-case/full_game.pad at its
`demo-record` label (year 3411's end browsing, before `research
slipgates`):

    make pad-author SCRIPT=worst-case/full_game.pad STOP_AT=demo-record OUT=tmp/demo_record.json
    make replay EVENTS=tmp/demo_record.json SAVE_HEX=pico8/demo_record.hex

pico8/demo_record.hex holds the 3852-byte record as one line of hex
(7705 bytes with its newline), sha256 61c39e56...83ae; re-cut, it is
byte-identical. Regenerating full_game never moves the demo: the hex
is frozen, and demo.pad reads it (`from-record demo_record.hex`).

    make pad-author                     (worst-case/ and this file)
    make pad-author SCRIPT=pico8/demo.pad OUT=pico8/demo.json
    make demo-check

pad_author writes pico8/demo.json without the aim budget, the record
as "initial_save" and "start": "resume"; frame 0 is the first frame
after the resume. make demo-check regenerates it byte for byte, then
replays it twice in one process (REPLAY_STARTS=2) and checks both ends
against the state at the `probe` label. Readings come from `make
state` of the file cut after the frame (the state at the end of that
frame) and from the framebuffer dump. The frames show no caption until
the attract mode draws them (plan 18 step 11). Buttons: 5 X, 6 O, 7
square, 10 R1, 12 Select.

The idles: the opening card is 300 frames, the longest reading moment
and the first impression. Each caption leads its command by 150
frames, room to read two lines (the rule asks 90), and stays 270
frames after it, while the outcome animates: the reveal, the colony,
the goods on the new slipway, the year box, the map after the panel.
The closing card is 180, the rule's size. One size per role keeps the
script regular: the parts differ only by their commands' frames (156
in all), 864 frames under the 3600 cap.
