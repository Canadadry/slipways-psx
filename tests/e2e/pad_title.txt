Pad only: pick "reasonable" on the title screen with the D-pad and X.
30 frames.

Pad input declared ("input": "pad"), no mouse field. Each press lasts
one frame and is followed by a release frame: a held direction moves
the focus once. Row colours below are read in the framebuffer dump
(make replay on the file cut after that frame), at x=108 of each row:
forgiving y53-61, reasonable y64-72, challenging y75-83, tough y86-94.

- f0-9: idle while the rows slide in. After f9: forgiving in colour 12,
  the other three in 13.
- f10: Down. After f10: reasonable 12, the others 13.
- f12: Down. After f12: challenging 12, the others 13.
- f14: Right, nothing to the right. After f14: challenging still 12.
- f16: Up. After f16 and f17: reasonable 12, the others 13.
- f18: X on "reasonable": the title rows are gone from f18, the game
  starts.
- f19-29: idle into the first month, in free mode: the cursor is the
  screen centre (80, 60), so its sprite tip sits at x=81, y=61.

`make state` at f29: clock year 3401 month 1, credits 100, planets 90,
colonies 0, slipways 0, structures 0, techs 0.

The end state has no difficulty line and does not tell difficulties
apart: the same file with X at f18 on each of the four rows gives
identical `make state` lines (planets included). The chosen difficulty
shows in the f17 dump above (reasonable is the only colour-12 row when
X is pressed). test_pad_nav checks the started game's
economy_score_difficulty().

Covers no keyboard_* scenario: keyboard_menus is covered by
pad_pause_restart (step 3.3).
