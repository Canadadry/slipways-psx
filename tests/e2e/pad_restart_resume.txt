Act, restart, then resume: pad_probe's probe, pan and fold, an idle
wait until the save holds the fold, Start, X on "restart", Up and X on
the title's "resume" row. The resumed game is the game before the
restart. 155 frames.

f0-76 are pad_probe's 77 frames verbatim: the first 77 frames of this
capture equal pad_probe's capture byte for byte (`cmp -n 739200`, both
start with an empty storage). Readings come from the capture's
framebuffer at each frame, and the state and the stored record from
`make state` / `make replay ... SAVE_HEX=<file>` of the file cut after
that frame. The hovered pause row is read at x=7, the title rows at
x=108, the crosshair at (78, 60) and (80, 58), as in
pad_pause_restart.txt.

- f0-76: as pad_probe. The stored record up to f83 is the probe's (year
  3401 month 1, credits 100): the fold is confirmed at f57, but the
  wormhole lives until f84 and no save is written while one lives.
- f84: the wormhole is gone, the end-of-frame save lands: month 2,
  credits 97, 90 nodes. It is the last write of the replay: the record
  is identical at f84, f111, f132 and f154.
- f85-108: the fold's red "-3" label (from f57) still rises over the
  map; gone from f109. f109-111: idle, nothing on screen but the map,
  the probe and its particles.
- f112: Start: the pause button colour 12 at (0, 111), the menu slides
  up, "restart" focused (y100-108 by f116), no crosshair under it.
- f118: X on "restart": the title. The "resume" row is drawn from f118
  (y42-50, colour 13), forgiving colour 12 from f119; the rows are
  settled from f126: resume y42-50, forgiving y53-61, reasonable y64-72,
  challenging y75-83, tough y86-94.
- f130: Up: "resume" colour 12, forgiving back to 13 (f130-131).
- f132: X on "resume": the title rows are gone on f132, the game is
  resumed during that frame's update; the crosshair is back from f133.
  f133-154: idle in free mode.

`make state` at f111, at f132 and at f154, the same lines apart from
zone and cap: clock year 3401 month 2, difficulty reasonable, credits
97, science 0, income 0, camera world 188 184, planets 90, colonies 0,
slipways 0, structures 0, techs 0, nodes 90. Known: p25 type f, p47
type r; the other 88 planets unknown.

Why not the PRD's plan (BEFORE=76, Start at f77): at f76 the wormhole
is still live, so the frame fails the save-frame conditions ("save
frame: a wormhole is live"), and the stored record is still the
probe's: a resume there brings back camera (176, 190), month 1, $100,
not the f76 game. Until f108 the "-3" label, which the save never
holds, differs from the resumed screen. So the recording, not the
check, moved: 35 idle frames (f77-111) after pad_probe's frames, Start
at f112, so that the frame before Start holds exactly what the record
holds.

Covers the act-restart-resume path: the title's "resume" row present
after a restart with a save on disk, Up onto it, X resuming the saved
game (camera, revealed planets, credits, clock, the probe). If a change
hid the row, Up would stay on forgiving, X would start a new game and
the hash would change.

No long-replay exception: 155 frames, within 180.

Cost: the replay alone about 80 ms (pad_probe's 77 frames about 43 ms),
on 4 cores.
