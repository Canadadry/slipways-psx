pad_colonise's first frames (probe, fold, a colony on p47, its
inspection panel opened and closed, aim mode and a fold, back to jump
mode on p47) played under swap_aim_menu.cfg, which swaps
jump.enter_aim and jump.open_menu (buttons 7 and 8). 175 frames.

The header names the fixture: "bindings": "swap_aim_menu.cfg", read by
replay from this directory. The fixture is bindings.default.cfg with
only those two jump lines changed, so every other mode keeps the
default table. Buttons 7 and 8 are exchanged only on the frames whose
press pad_colonise reads in jump mode; every other frame is
pad_colonise's, byte for byte.

Swapped (pad_colonise's button -> this file's):

- f135: 8 -> 7, jump mode: jump.open_menu on the hovered colony; its
  inspection panel opens on the f136 release.
- f141: 8 -> 7, jump mode: jump.open_menu again; the panel opens on
  the f142 release.
- f146: 7 -> 8, jump mode: jump.enter_aim, aim mode over p47.

Kept, because the fixture leaves these modes on the default:

- f16: 7 in aim mode, aim.leave_aim, nothing known yet: does nothing.
- f86: 7 in aim mode, aim.leave_aim: jump mode on p47.
- f144: 8 in menu mode, menu.open_menu: closes the inspection panel.
  A swap leaking from jump into menu or aim turns this file red.

The mode of each press is the one it is read in, before the frame
runs, from the mode log of pad_colonise's frames proof alone:

    ./tmp/pad_author tools/pad_author_proofs/pad_colonise.frames.pad \
        tmp/pad-author/pad_colonise.frames.json --no-aim-budget

(its JSON is tests/e2e/pad_colonise.json; the log is
tmp/pad-author/pad_colonise.modes.txt, which `make pad-author-proof`
overwrites with the end-state proof's log). Its lines `f<N> jump`
pressing 7 or 8 up to f174 are exactly f135, f141 and f146. f175, a
jump-mode 7, is past the cut. Every 7 or 8 press here is a one-frame
tap, so no hold starts in jump mode and continues in another.

Every captured frame is identical to pad_colonise's first 175 frames,
so pad_colonise.txt's readings for f0-174 apply, and this file's
.json.sha256 is the hash of pad_colonise cut after f174. With the
fixture's swap undone, the frames diverge from f135; the end state
does not, because it records neither the camera nor the nav mode: the
.sha256 is the proof of the remap, not the .end-state.

`make state` at f174: clock year 3401 month 6, credits 89, income -1,
colonies 1, slipways 0, structures 0, techs 0. p47 colonized type r,
p25 known type f; every other planet unknown.
