A second fold and colony, the old slipway drag in aim mode with what
only it draws (partial line and cost, snap on a known planet, a crossed
planet, a drop on empty space, a fold under a drag, a drag pressed from

It is the regression guard for the drag the pad once lacked (a blind
press-release gesture drew nothing while aiming). 308 frames.

Jump mode is the map's root, where every game starts: square on f13
enters aim mode, the camera unmoved. pad_probe's f16-17 (O back to jump
mode with nothing hovered, square back to aim mode) are shared.

the galaxy and pad_probe.txt's planet list apply, and p47 (type r) is
colony A. In aim mode the cursor is the screen centre (80, 60), marked
by the colour-7 crosshair (x78-82 on y=60, y58-62 on x=80, the centre
pixel undrawn). The dragged line is the slipway's core, two pixels

slipway_display.c). Labels are colour 8 (targeting_tooltip_show,

camera has no velocity in pad mode, so it stops there. Readings below
come from the framebuffer of each frame (the capture, or the dump and

keyboard_slipway clicks the title at f1 and a pad title needs the rows
