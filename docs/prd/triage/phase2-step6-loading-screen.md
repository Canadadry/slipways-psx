## Problem Statement

When the player picks a difficulty on the title screen, the game generates
the galaxy (`galaxy_populate`, called by `cart_start_game`) inside the
press handler, in the same frame's update. On the judge that frame is the
worst of `full_game.json`: frame 12, update 3,711 hblanks, 15 vsyncs. During
those vsyncs the screen keeps the last title picture, frozen with the
difficulty rows still on it, so the game looks hung. Plan 22 keeps the
stall (spreading it over frames is out of scope) but wants it to look
intended: a black screen with the word "loading" in a corner, no animation.

Three things make this more than a draw call:

- **The picture on screen during the stall is not the frame being
  computed.** The PC presents each frame right after `cart_draw`, so during
  frame N's update the PC shows frame N−1. The PSX double-buffers:
  `psx_gpu_frame_end` waits for the previous frame's drawing, displays the
  previous frame's buffer, then starts drawing this one, so during frame N's
  update the PSX shows frame N−2. Today both show the title.
- **It must be the shared game code**, so PC and PSX stay identical, and it
  is the plan's only allowed change to the picture and to the e2e hashes. The
  game played after the press must be the same game: every `.end-state` in
  `tests/e2e/` and `worst-case/` unchanged.
- **Frame numbers are keys everywhere.** Adding frames shifts every frame of
  every recording that starts a game, and `full_game.json`'s numbering is
  the key of the committed frame table, the frozen situation table and its
  hand-picked test frames, the short-run windows, the probe baseline, the
  GPU capture list of `pad_slipway`, the e2e notes, `pad-metrics-check`'s
  frames and several unit tests. The check-e2e skill forbids
  `e2e-baseline`; this step is the plan's single sanctioned re-record.

## Solution

**The game.** Choosing a difficulty no longer generates the galaxy at once.
The press records the chosen difficulty and the game enters a loading
state. The frame of the press and the frame after it draw the loading
screen: black, "loading" in colour 7 in the bottom-right corner, nothing
else, the same pixels on both frames. The frame after those two generates
the galaxy at the top of its update, exactly as the press used to, and draws
the new game's first map frame. So the frame on screen during the stall is
the loading picture on PC (frame N−1) and on PSX (frame N−2). The two
loading frames run no game code: no entity update, no UI, no save, no random
draw, so the random state at the generation, and so the galaxy and the whole
game, are the same as before. Resuming a saved game (no generation) and the
attract demo (it resumes a record) are unchanged.

**The recordings.** Every recording that starts a game from the title gets
two input frames inserted after each difficulty press, copies of the press
frame, so the game sees every later input at the same point of play. The
worst-case games are regenerated by `pad_author`, which holds the press
through the loading frames by the same rule. The e2e captures are
re-recorded once, after a mechanical proof that each new capture is the old
one with the two loading frames inserted and every `.end-state` unchanged.

**The renumbering.** `full_game.json` grows from 6,059 to 6,061 frames;
every frame from the old 12 on moves by +2 (old f ≥ 12 → new f + 2; the new
frames 12 and 13 are the loading frames). The situation table is
regenerated (the frozen rule labels the loading frames `title`), the
hand-picked frames move with it, the short-run windows move, the GPU
capture list of `pad_slipway` moves and gains two captures, and
`psx-full-game-check` measures the new frame table that replaces
`psx/perf/full-run-frames.txt`.

**The PSX display proof.** A new kind of GPU capture, `display`, takes a
screenshot of what the PSX is displaying at the top of a frame, before its
update, without touching the display. Taken at the generation frame of
`pad_slipway`, it must equal the PC's loading frame at `differ 0`.

## Implementation Decisions

### Why two loading frames, and where the generation goes

- A frame is: input step, `cart_update60`, `cart_draw`, then the platform
  presents. PC (`src/cmd/game.c`, `raylib_backend_present`) shows frame N
  from the end of frame N; the PSX (`psx_gpu_frame_end`: `DrawSync`, then
  `PutDispEnv` of the other buffer, then `DrawOTag` of this one) shows frame
  N from the end of frame N+1. During frame N's update, the PC shows N−1
  and the PSX N−2.
- So with the press at frame L: L and L+1 draw the loading screen, L+2's
  update generates. PC shows L+1 during the stall, PSX shows L. Both are the
  loading picture. One loading frame would be enough on PC only. The plan's
  "the extra frame" becomes two frames; the plan's verification sentence
  ("the PSX shows each frame one frame late") is what requires the second.
- The count is a named constant of the loading module (2). Its declaration
  says, in one line, that the PSX displays each frame one frame late, so 1
  would show the title during the stall on PSX.
- The platform frame loops (`game.c`, `psx_game.c`, `psx_gpu_replay.c`,
  `replay.c` and the other drivers) do not change. Presenting a frame
  earlier on the PSX would give up the CPU/GPU overlap every frame, a
  performance loss the plan forbids.

### The game (`src/game/`)

- **Loading module** (new, `game/loading_screen`): `loading_screen_draw(gfx,
  mem)` draws the picture; it owns the text, the corner, the colour and the
  frame count. Picture: `p8_cls` to colour 0, then `p8_print` of
  `"loading"` in colour 7, right and bottom margins of 2 pixels, computed
  from `P8_SCREEN_WIDTH`/`P8_SCREEN_HEIGHT` and the font's 4-pixel advance
  and 5-pixel height (on 160×120: x 132, y 114). Plain print, no outline, no
  text image, no sprite, no fill pattern. It sets the camera to 0,0, no clip
  and the default palette for its own drawing, and restores the PICO-8 draw
  state it found (camera, clip, palettes, pen colour, print cursor, fill
  pattern) before returning.
- **Cart** (`game/cart`):
  - `cart_request_start_game(button_t *chosen)`: the new bridge the title's
    difficulty buttons call. It copies the chosen option's value (as
    `cart_resume_game` builds a `button_t` value; `NULL` stays `NULL`) into
    cart-owned storage and enters the loading state with the press frame
    counted as the first loading frame. It never touches the world.
  - `cart_load` installs `cart_request_start_game` with
    `help_set_start_game`; `cart_init`'s assert on the bridge names it.
  - `cart_start_game(button_t *)` stays as it is: start the world, generate,
    start the interface, recompute the economy, synchronously. Unit tests,
    `largest_slots` and the deferred start call it.
  - `cart_update60` while loading: on the second loading frame it returns at
    once (no frame zone reset, no exit-queue flush, no entity update, no UI,
    no save). On the frame after, it resets the frame zone, flushes the exit
    queue, calls `cart_start_game` with the stored value, leaves the loading
    state, then runs only `save_at_frame_end` (as the press frame used to
    after its handler): no `update_entities`, no `ui_update` that frame.
  - `cart_draw` while loading (the press frame and the next) calls
    `loading_screen_draw` and nothing else: no `text_image_frame_begin`, no
    `draw_layers`, no demo overlay.
  - `cart_loading()` (new, read-only): 1 from the press to the end of the
    second loading frame, else 0. `pad_author` and the tests read it.
  - `cart_title_shown()` is 0 while loading.
  - `cart_load`, `cart_init` and `cart_return_to_title` clear the loading
    state.
- **Judge of "the same game"**: the `.end-state` of every recording in
  `tests/e2e/` and `worst-case/`, compared by `make end-state`. If one
  differs, the deferred start does not reproduce what the old press frame
  did after the handler (the tail of `ui_update` on the title UI, then the
  save); the implementer finds which state that tail touched and reproduces
  it in the generation frame. The end-state files are never re-baselined.
- `src/devtools/situation.c`, the labeling rule and `pad_metrics`' screen
  modes are not changed. A loading frame has no economy, so its mode is
  `title`, and with no event and a steady previous frame the frozen rule
  labels it `title`, family `steady`.

### The recordings

- **Insertion rule**: after each frame whose input chooses a difficulty
  (the press that calls `cart_request_start_game`), insert two frames that
  copy that frame's input exactly (pad buttons, and for any non-pad fixture
  its mouse fields too). The input step then sees the same edges the game
  saw before: the press, then the change to the next recorded frame, which
  the game reads in its first update after the generation, as before. Held
  durations grow by two frames, under any repeat delay.
- **`pad_author`**: `run_title` (used by `title` and `new-game`) emits the
  choose-row press, then repeats it while `cart_loading()` is 1 (at most two
  frames, else it fails naming the frame), then checks the game started, as
  today. Absolute frame numbers written in `.pad` scripts after a press
  move by +2 per earlier generation where a script uses them;
  `worst-case/*.pad` comments that quote a generation frame are updated.
- **Regenerated by `make pad-author`**: `worst-case/full_game.json`,
  `full_game_new_game.json`, `full_game_pause_restart.json`,
  `full_game_pause_idle.json`. `pico8/demo.json` (from a record) must come
  out byte-identical. `title_idle.json` and `attract_cycle.json` (no
  generation) are not touched, and `make demo-check` must pass with
  `attract_cycle.json.sha256` unchanged.
- **Adjusted by the rule** (a throwaway script, not committed): every e2e
  of `tests/e2e/` that generates a galaxy, and the recordings in
  `tests/fixtures/` that pick a difficulty (`title_pick_reasonable.json`,
  `title_pick_forgiving.json`, any other one a test plays through a press).
  An e2e with a `tools/pad_author_proofs/<e2e>.frames.pad` must equal what
  `pad_author` writes from it (`make pad-author-proof`), so the two paths
  agree.
- **Insertion check** (once, output in the note): for every recording
  changed, the new file equals the old one with the two copies inserted
  after each press, nothing else changed. A short throwaway script is
  enough; it is not committed.
- **Expected e2e changes**: exactly the e2e whose replay generates a galaxy
  (from the title, or after a restart or "again"). Expected from the
  headers: `pad_build`, `pad_colonise`, `pad_empty_planet`, `pad_end_turn`,
  `pad_game_end`, `pad_message_box`, `pad_pause_restart`, `pad_probe`,
  `pad_research`, `pad_restart_resume`, `pad_score`, `pad_slipway`,
  `pad_swap_aim_menu`, `pad_title`; unchanged: `attract`, `pad_bankrupt`,
  `pad_planet_projects`, `pad_resume_at_start`, `pad_tech_costs`,
  `pad_unbound_enter_aim` (they start from an `initial_save`). The rule
  decides, not this list: the note gives both lists as measured, and a
  difference from this one is explained.
- **Capture check before the re-record** (once, output in the note): `make
  e2e` red on exactly the expected files; for each, the new capture
  (`tmp/frames/<e2e>.bin`, 9,600 bytes a frame) equals the old one (`make
  e2e-ref`'s reference capture) with two loading frames inserted at each
  press frame index, and those inserted frames equal the loading picture.
  Only then `make e2e-baseline`, once. After it, `make e2e` is green and
  the `.sha256` diff touches only the expected files.
- **Notes and limits**: each changed e2e `.txt` gets its frame count and
  every frame number after a press moved (+2 per earlier generation), and a
  line saying frames L and L+1 are the loading screen.
  `tools/e2e_long_exceptions.txt`'s `N frames:` follow. No e2e crosses 180
  frames (largest affected: `pad_swap_aim_menu`, 175 → 177).
- **Frame-keyed checks outside the recordings**: `pad-metrics-check`'s
  required group (`f16`, `f17`, `f293` of `pad_colonise`) moves to `f18`,
  `f19`, `f295`. Unit tests that stop a recording at a frame number
  (`test_live_slots`' `pad_pause_restart` frames, and any other test the
  suite turns red) move their frames; a test that froze "the press frame
  starts the game" is updated to the loading behavior, as the CLAUDE.md
  rule on e2e being the truth says.

### The renumbering of `full_game.json`

- **Mapping**: old frame f < 12 → f; old f ≥ 12 → f + 2. New 12 and 13 are
  the loading frames. 6,059 → 6,061 frames.
- **Situation table**: regenerated by `make situations`, then committed as
  `psx/perf/full-run-situations.txt`; `make situations-check` green. Check
  (once, in the note): the new table equals the old one with `12 title
  steady` and `13 title steady` inserted and every later line renumbered by
  the mapping.
- **Hand-picked frames** (`test_situation_table`): every frame moves by the
  mapping (galaxy-generation at 14, map-move 32, ..., game-end 6045),
  `FULL_GAME_FRAMES` = 6,061, and `{12, "title"}`, `{13, "title"}` are
  added with a comment naming the loading screen.
- **Short-run manifest** (`psx/short_runs.txt`): every window frame and
  every frame quoted in a `# no representative run:` comment moves by the
  mapping (`start prefix 14 14 101`, `map-move-1057 resume 1059 1073`, ...).
  **Names are kept**: a name records the window's first frame when the run
  was created; `docs/psx-game.md` § Short runs says so in one sentence.
  The cut rule is unchanged; the resumed runs' `run.json` hold the same
  game frames, so their windows are expected to give the same figures.
- **Frame table**: one `make psx-full-game-check` (asserts off, O3) with the
  new situation table in the working tree (its `real-game-budget` joins
  them), its `frames.txt` replacing `psx/perf/full-run-frames.txt`, both
  tables committed in the same commit. Every condition but `frame-budget`
  and `real-game-budget`'s steady part must hold, as at step 1.
- **Figures for the note**: `make situation-report` on the new tables;
  `make psx-real-game-budget`; the one-off line must read vsyncs ≤ 1,391;
  the steady line next to step 1's 1,133 (expected equal: the loading
  frames are `title`, far under 263). A comparison of the two frame tables
  under the mapping: frames whose total differs, by how much, and the new
  frames 12–14 against the old frame 12.
- **Short runs again**: `make short-run-situations` (every steady run
  `same`), then `make psx-short-runs` against the new full-game table: the
  three representative runs `representative ok`; the three best tries keep
  their `# no representative run:` lines with their new gaps; slipway-aim
  keeps its mark. A representative run that turns red is a finding to
  explain, never a reason to change a window or a mark. The plan 21 runs
  (`start`, `update-*`, `draw-*`) need only `done`, `deterministic`,
  `breakdown` and `asserts`.
- **Probe baseline**: `make psx-probe-baseline` after the commit of the new
  tables (its fingerprint holds `full_game.json`'s blob), every run valid.
  `psx/probes/*.patch` are refreshed if the cart change moves their context
  (`delay-update-and-draw.patch` patches `cart_update60` and `cart_draw`).
- **Other frame-keyed files**: `psx/perf/image-build-frames.txt` (frame
  indexes of the judge table, read by no target) is renumbered by the
  mapping. `docs/psx-game.md`: every quoted `full_game.json` frame number
  moves (frame 12 → 14 for galaxy generation, the short-run window table,
  "Still to do", the situation section's "frame 12 is galaxy-generation"),
  the "6,059" counts become 6,061, the latest-run figures come from the new
  table, and the Situations section says the frozen table went through step
  6's renumbering once. The plan's own step notes are history and are not
  edited.
- `psx/perf/draw-functions.txt` gains `loading_screen_draw` in group
  `frame`, so a profile that meets the loading frames never fails on an
  unknown symbol.

### PSX: the `display` capture and the captures of `pad_slipway`

- **`display` entries** in `psx/gpu_captures.txt`: `display <F> display`.
  The label and the mask are both the word `display`; `capture_list`
  accepts it as a mask of its own, alone, with F ≥ 2, at most one entry per
  frame.
  - GPU replay: at the top of frame F, before the input step and the
    update, it runs the capture handshake without drawing and without
    `psx_gpu_frame_show_last`: the display is left as the frame loop set it.
    The Lua (`psx_capture_frames.lua`) writes the screenshot as it does for
    the other captures. Its time and vsyncs stay out of the frame's, as the
    frame-locked handshake's do.
  - PC side (`tmp/replay` with `CAPTURE_LIST=`): the entry's PC frame is
    frame F−2 drawn unfiltered, the PSX display model. The host test of the
    cart (below) proves frames F−2 and F−1 are the same loading picture, so
    the PC's own display (F−1) is covered too.
  - Conditions: `gpu-capture-list` exempts `display` from the family rule,
    as `all`; `gpu-captures`, `-deterministic`, `-clean`, `-report`,
    `-ceilings`, `-references` treat it as any capture. Its reference is
    accepted with `make psx-gpu-capture-accept LABEL=display` under the
    agent rule (every capture of the label at `differ 0`).
  - It covers `psx-game` too: both programs end each frame with the same
    `psx_gpu_frame_end`, the only place the display changes.
- **Chunk 1 proves it on today's game**: `display 14 display` on
  `pad_slipway` (press and generation at f12) shows the map of f12 at
  `differ 0`, and is accepted. After the game change, the same entry shows
  the loading frame (f12), at `differ 0`, and is re-accepted.
- **Loading frame capture**: `all 12 all` (the press frame, the one the PSX
  displays during the stall). `all` because the picture is cls and print

## Testing Decisions

- A good test drives the game through its public entry points
  (`cart_load`, `cart_init`, a played input frame through `input_step_run`,
  `cart_update60`, `cart_draw`) and checks what a player or a tool sees:
  the framebuffer, whether a game world exists, the state lines. It never
  reads the cart's private loading fields.
- **New unit test of the loading screen** (in `make test`, under 50 ms, no
  recording replayed): on a fresh cart at the title, a difficulty press
  through the input step; then: after the press frame's update no economy
  exists; the press frame's framebuffer and the next one's are each the
  loading picture (every pixel 0 except the word's, which match a
  `p8_print` of "loading" in colour 7 at the computed corner on a black
  screen); after the second loading frame's update still no economy; after
  the third frame's update the economy exists with the chosen difficulty;
  the third frame's framebuffer is not the loading picture. Also: input
  during the two loading frames changes nothing; `cart_title_shown` is 0
  while loading; the random state before the third frame's update equals
  the one right after the press frame's update. Prior art: `tests/test_cart.c`,
  `tests/test_help_screen.c`, `tests/test_input_step.c`.
- **Display model in the same test**: the frame on display during the
  generation frame's update is frame F−1 on PC and F−2 on PSX; both are
  asserted equal to the loading picture. The constant's one-line reason is
  the trap this test guards.
- **`pad_author`**: `make pad-author-self-check` (in `make test`) and `make
  pad-author-proof` stay green; a `run_title` that does not see the game
  start within two loading frames fails with a message.
- **`capture_list`**: its existing unit test gains the `display` cases
  (accepted alone; refused with another mask name, at F < 2, twice on one
  frame).
- **Capture protocol / Lua**: the existing constant check
  (`test_psx_capture_protocol`) covers any new state word.
- **Situation table**: `test_situation_table` with the moved and added
  hand-picked frames.
- **Unit tests that froze the old frame order** are updated, not deleted;
  their intent (what the test checked at the press frame) moves to the
  generation frame.
- **No new e2e.** The re-recorded e2e are the proof of the picture; the
  `.end-state` files are the proof of the game.
- Nothing slow enters `make test` or `make e2e`. The two inserted frames
  per generation are the only added cost there (about thirty frames over the
  whole e2e suite), which the plan's decision implies.

  which a frame presents.

- Levers, the lever list's content and ranking beyond the renumbering

  loading frames.
